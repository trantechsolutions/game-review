# DFC Game Film: What's New

Plain-language notes on what changed on the team's game review site, newest first.

## 2026-10-23 — 19 September game (ca79a09..54381d1)

- The 19 September scrimmage against CFC (lost 1–4) is now on the site and in NOLA DFC Manager, with the play-by-play, match report, coaches' breakdown, unit review and five highlight clips, in English and Spanish.
- The game also shows up on the 19 September entry in the manager's schedule.

## 2026-10-22 — Publishing into NOLA DFC Manager (833ddac..833ddac)

- The season overview, game library, match reports and highlight clips now open inside NOLA DFC Manager, with no password. Everyone on the team can see them, and coaches also see the recurring themes and who played where.
- Highlight clips play straight from the manager and start faster, because they no longer have to be downloaded and unlocked first.
- The play-by-play, unit review, coaches' breakdown, player pages and the scorekeeper are still on this site for now.

## 2026-10-21 — NOLA DFC Manager connection (129c8c6..129c8c6)

- The game film site now opens straight from NOLA DFC Manager, with no password to type. Families see the games. Coaches also get the coach area. Anyone given the Scorekeeper role also gets the scorekeeper screen.
- In the manager's schedule, games that have been broken down now show **Match report** and **Highlights** buttons.
- Setting up a new game in the scorekeeper is one tap: pick it from the team schedule and the opponent, date, kickoff and venue fill in.
- Stats kept from a manager account are recorded under that person's name. If the account loses its role, nothing on the phone is lost. It asks you to open Keep stats from the manager again.
- Each player's season numbers (minutes, starts, goals, assists, shots, cards) now appear on their profile in the manager, including for their parents.
- The passwords still work for anyone without a manager account.

## 2026-10-20 — Ollie game history and a clearer game layout (b9ef61f..b9ef61f)

- The site now opens on the season overview. The list of reviewed games is its own tab, "Game film library".
- New **Game history** tab: 108 earlier games from Ollie Sports, back to September 2023, that the library doesn't already cover. Each shows the result, possession, shots and shots on goal. A record panel (wins, draws, losses, goals, home and away) updates as you filter by season or opponent. Tapping a game shows its full numbers by half. Coaches also see each player's numbers.
- Every game's pages now share one clear header (score, date, scorers, and the games before and after) with tabs that stay at the top. A game opens on the match report.
- In the library, each game has a play button for highlights and a paper button for the match report, right under the date.

## 2026-10-20 — Scorekeeper mode, lineups and season overview (f0146ce..f0146ce)

- There is now a scorekeeper screen for keeping stats live at the game, from a phone held sideways. It has its own password and opens nothing else on the site.
- The scorekeeper follows the ball: tap the next player to get it for a pass, or the other team when they win it. Swipe a player for a shot, cross or clearance. There are buttons for the ball going out and for fouls and cards. Shots are marked on a picture of the goal, and corners, goal kicks and throw-ins on the touchline or goal line where the ball went out.
- Stats can also be called out loud: "9 to 17, shot saved", "foul by them", "sub Adam for Jayden", "Dee moves to striker".
- Substitutions and position changes can be made from a lineup screen, by dragging, or by voice.
- Everything is saved on the phone and sent securely once there is signal, so a lost connection at the field loses nothing. Test games can be deleted.
- For coaches: each game can now carry its lineup, there is a squad page with every player's minutes and starts, and a season overview page.

## 2026-10-12 — Positions as acronyms (55fa811..55fa811)

- Player positions are now written the way coaches say them: GK, LB, LCB, RCB, RB, LM, RM, CM, ST and so on, in English and Spanish.

## 2026-10-12 — Unit review page (6c36dbb..6c36dbb)

- Every game has a new **Unit review** page, in English and Spanish. It looks at the defence, the midfield and the attack separately: what went well, what went wrong, and how to improve, such as marking, reading the play, body shape and formation.
- Each point links to the moment in the game it comes from, so it's easy to find on the video. Coaches can leave notes on each unit.
